# Psalm 29 — unpublished whole-chapter candidate

Eleven exact bundled BSB verses, David's heading, seven voice declarations and the oaks/Glory wording in verse 9 are preserved. Original individual Meaning/Context/Explore/Reflect notes follow the heavenly invitation, storm imagery, enduring kingship and closing communal strength/peace blessing. All twenty-two related references were read with surrounding bundled verses before drafting; Ezekiel 31 was read in full to distinguish its political cedar allegory. No historical storm is invented, no weather event is labeled punishment, and God is not pictured as a human body.

Eleven distinct watercolors were generated with the built-in image_gen.imagegen tool using assets/psalm23/verse-2-watercolor.webp as the style reference. Each original and production WebP was visually inspected. Full prompts and original filenames are in assets/psalm29/generation-prompts.json. Production images are 1024×683 WebP at quality 80, totaling 2,235,908 bytes (2.13 MiB), packaged offline and for Android. Two empty conversion files were rebuilt from their saved originals before validation; all eleven final files are nonempty and inspected.

Integration preserves shared Saved/dialog reflections, isolated Psalm 28/29 resume, David, gratitude/fear/strength connections and 28→29→30 navigation. Story manuscripts, scene data, renderers and original Psalm 23 are unchanged. Publication requires passing source/reference/asset/offline/parity checks, every verse/tab/image at 390px on source website and packaged webview, reflection reload, resume, topic links, transitions, Capacitor, PR CI, authorized merge, main CI, Pages and live verification. Native APK/device testing is separate.

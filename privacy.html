Scripture for Life

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Applies to the website at dvdjoe-sudo.github.io/scripture-for-life and the Android app Scripture for Life (package com.scriptureforlife.bible).

Summary

Scripture for Life is a Bible reading and study app. It does not require an account, does not sell personal information, and does not operate its own analytics, advertising, or user-tracking backend. Notes and bookmarks stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app and website store the following in local browser / WebView storage on your device only:

  • Saved verses (bookmarks) and optional personal reflection notes
  • Reading place / resume markers (for example last reader chapter or story scene)
  • Theme preference (light / dark) and text-size preference
  • Simple reading streak or progress markers used in the interface

This data is not uploaded to Scripture for Life servers. The project does not provide cloud sync or accounts. Clearing site or app storage, uninstalling the app, or using another device removes or isolates that data. Other people who use the same browser profile or device may be able to see locally stored notes.

Internet use

Based on the current code:

  • First-party content: Bible text, stories, art, and scripts are packaged with the website or Android web bundle. Opening chapters loads bundled data/bible.json (and related assets) from the app or site itself—not from a third-party Bible API.
  • Optional outbound links: Some About and study pages link to public sources such as berean.bible or biblehub.com. Following those links leaves this app and is governed by those sites’ policies.
  • Sharing: If you use Share, the Android system share sheet may send content you choose to another app you pick. Scripture for Life does not receive that share.
  • Service worker / GitHub Pages (website): The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading or updating the site uses GitHub’s hosting infrastructure under GitHub’s terms.

Text-to-speech (Listen)

Listen uses your device’s built-in Web Speech / text-to-speech voices when available. Availability and pronunciation vary by device. Some device voices may use the device maker’s online speech service; that behavior is controlled by the operating system or browser, not by a Scripture for Life server. You can read all text without using Listen.

Permissions (Android)

The Android app requests Internet so the WebView can load packaged assets and so that optional system TTS or outbound links can work when the device needs network access. The app does not use a local-notifications plugin and does not schedule push or daily reminder notifications in the current code.

Children

The app is a general audience Bible reader. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account registration.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised page will be published on the GitHub Pages site.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the scripture-for-life GitHub repository (owner: dvdjoe-sudo).

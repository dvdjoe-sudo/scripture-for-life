Scripture for Life

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). Updated the same day for the 0.6.0 Android APK audit and user-controlled backup. Applies to the website at dvdjoe-sudo.github.io/scripture-for-life and the Android app Scripture for Life (package com.scriptureforlife.bible).

Summary

Scripture for Life is completely free. There are no ads, no data tracking or analytics, and no sign-in or accounts. Notes, bookmarks, and preferences stay on your device only unless you choose to export a backup file somewhere you pick. The project does not sell personal information and does not operate its own analytics, advertising, or user-tracking backend.

Information stored on your device

The app and website store the following in local browser / WebView storage (localStorage keys prefixed with scripture-for-life.) on your device:

  • Saved verses (bookmarks / favorites) and optional personal reflection notes
  • Story-scene reflection notes and episode progress markers
  • Reading place / resume markers (for example last reader chapter or story scene)
  • Theme preference (light / dark) and text-size preference
  • Simple reading streak, autoplay, topic-scroll, and related UI preferences

This data is not uploaded to Scripture for Life servers. The project does not provide cloud sync or accounts. Clearing site or app storage, uninstalling the app, or using another device removes or isolates that data. Other people who use the same browser profile or device may be able to see locally stored notes.

On Android, the app allows the system Auto Backup flag (android:allowBackup="true"). If you have device backup enabled, Android may include app WebView / local storage in your personal device backups (for example to your Google account). That is controlled by the operating system and your backup settings, not by a Scripture for Life server.

User-controlled backup (export / import)

From About, you can Export backup to create a JSON file of all scripture-for-life.* local data, then use your device’s share sheet or file save to store it in Google Drive, Dropbox, email, Files, or another place you choose. Scripture for Life does not provide a built-in cloud backup service and does not receive that file. Import backup restores from a file you select, after validation and your confirmation that existing local data may be replaced. Only backups that match this app’s format are accepted.

Internet use

Based on an audit of the 0.6.0 Android web bundle and website code (no third-party CDN fonts, analytics SDKs, ads, or tracking pixels are loaded by this project):

  • First-party content: Bible text, stories, art, and scripts are packaged with the website or Android web bundle. Opening chapters loads bundled data/bible.json (and related assets) from the app or site itself—not from a third-party Bible API.
  • Optional outbound links: Some About, reader, and study pages link to public sources including berean.bible, bereanbible.com, and biblehub.com. Following those links leaves this app and is governed by those sites’ policies.
  • Sharing: If you use Share (verses or a backup), the Android Capacitor Share plugin or system share sheet may send content you choose to another app you pick. Verse shares may include a public website URL (https://dvdjoe-sudo.github.io/scripture-for-life/). Scripture for Life does not receive that share.
  • Service worker / GitHub Pages (website): The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading or updating the site uses GitHub’s hosting infrastructure under GitHub’s terms. The Android WebView does not register the service worker on its local Capacitor host.

Text-to-speech (Listen)

Listen uses your device’s built-in Web Speech / text-to-speech voices when available. Availability and pronunciation vary by device. Some device voices may use the device maker’s online speech service; that behavior is controlled by the operating system or browser, not by a Scripture for Life server. You can read all text without using Listen.

Permissions (Android)

The merged 0.6.0 APK declares Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) so the WebView can load packaged assets and so that optional system TTS or outbound links can work when the device needs network access. Build tooling may also add a package-private signature permission used only by the app itself (…DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION); it is not a user-facing access grant. The app does not include a local-notifications plugin and does not schedule push or daily reminder notifications in the current code. There are no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage-access permissions in the merged manifest.

Children

The app is a general audience Bible reader. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account registration.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the revised page will be published on the GitHub Pages site.

# Psalm 1 verse-by-verse artwork

Six original watercolor illustrations made with the built-in image generation tool, matching the user-selected soft watercolor Psalm 23:2 preview. Production files are `verse-1-watercolor.webp` through `verse-6-watercolor.webp`, 1280 × 853 pixels, WebP quality 82. All are bundled for offline website use and Android.

The complete generation prompts are recorded in `generation-prompts.json`. The scenes depict: choosing a path; a scroll and lamp at dawn; a fruitful tree beside streams; windblown chaff; an imagined assembly on firm ground; and two contrasting ways through the landscape.

These are symbolic pictures of the poem's imagery. Figures are not portraits of an identified biblical character. The assembly is not a depiction of God judging named people. No historical setting is asserted.

Verse-specific editorial explanations, context, imagery notes, reflections, and related Bible passages are in `psalm1.js`. The exact Scripture is read from the existing complete BSB source rather than reproduced in the study configuration.

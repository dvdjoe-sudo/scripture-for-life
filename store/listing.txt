FULL_DESCRIPTION:
Scripture for Life is completely free. There are no ads, no data tracking or analytics, and no sign-in or accounts. Your notes, bookmarks, and preferences stay on your device only.

It is a quiet place to meet God’s Word. Find encouragement for everyday needs—fear, grief, strength, forgiveness, and more—through curated Berean Standard Bible (BSB) passages. Read the complete 66-book BSB in a simple chapter reader. Explore illustrated Psalms, story journeys, and Bible people profiles that link straight to the verses they summarize.

Features
• Completely free — no ads, no tracking, no account
• Eight “everyday needs” topic guides with Scripture in context
• Full BSB reader (public-domain Berean Standard Bible)
• Illustrated Psalms chapters and selected verse studies
• Story journeys (including Genesis and other books as available)
• People profiles with step-by-step scripture links
• Save verses and write private reflections on your device
• Export / import a JSON backup you save where you choose (Drive, Files, email, etc.) — no built-in cloud service
• Dark mode and larger text
• Listen with your device’s text-to-speech when available

Your data stays on your device unless you export a backup yourself. Scripture for Life does not sell your data.

Bible text: Berean Standard Bible (BSB), public domain. Study notes and artwork are original editorial additions, separate from Scripture.
